How It Works

Card Flip for Stats

Click anywhere on the card to flip it and reveal detailed stats with animated bars. Click again to flip back. The stat bars animate in when the card flips.

Form Switcher

Click the purple "3 forms" badge to cycle through different forms (Base, Mega X, Mega Y, etc.). The sprite, name, types, and stats all update to match the selected form.
